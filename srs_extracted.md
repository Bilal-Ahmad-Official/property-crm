“PMS CRM”
# 1. Product Objective
Build a centralized real-estate business management platform that can organize property inventory, customers, bookings, sales, purchases, financial transactions, transfers, buy-back activities users and management reporting in one workspace.
# 2. Target Users
System administrators responsible for configuration and access.
Sales and booking staff managing customers and transactions.
Management users reviewing approvals, summaries and reports.
Finance/operations staff handling payments and transaction records.
Property/inventory staff maintaining property information.
# 3. Functional Scope
Dashboard & Analytics
Property & Inventory
Customer Management
Booking Management
Sales Management
Purchase Management
Payments & Receipts
Property Transfer
Buy-Back Management
Charges & Notices
Reports
User Administration
Configuration
# 4. Suggested Business Lifecycle
Create a business record.
Capture and validate required information.
Submit the record for internal review.
Perform verification/review steps where required.
Approve, reject or return the record for correction.
Generate or maintain related financial/operational records.
Make the resulting information available in dashboards and reports.
# 5. Property & Inventory Requirements
Property records should contain enough information to identify and classify an inventory item.
Support organizational grouping such as projects/blocks or other business-defined categories.
Provide an inventory view showing the current business state of properties.
Allow authorized users to maintain property-related master information.
Support availability/status visibility for operational decision-making.
# 6. Transaction Requirements
Transactions should have unique business references.
Transactions should maintain relationships with relevant customers, properties and financial records.
Important transaction changes should be traceable through status/history information.
The system should prevent invalid duplicate or conflicting transactions where business rules require it.
Completed or approved records should have appropriate edit restrictions.
# 7. Financial Requirements
Support transaction amounts and applicable additional charges.
Maintain payment/receipt records against relevant transactions.
Show outstanding and received amounts where applicable.
Provide management summaries for financial activity.
Support configurable currency/display preferences.
# 8. Approval & Control Requirements
The platform should support controlled review of sensitive business transactions. Different transaction types may require different approval responsibilities.
Role-based access to business areas.
Controlled approval responsibilities.
Rejection with a reason where appropriate.
Audit/activity history for important actions.
Protection against unauthorized modification of finalized records.
# 9. Reporting Requirements
Inventory and property summaries.
Booking and sales summaries.
Purchase summaries.
Payment/receipt information.
Outstanding/balance information.
Operational activity and approval-related reporting.
Filtering and search appropriate to each report.
# 10. User Experience Requirements
Clear navigation between functional areas.
Search and filtering for large record sets.
Responsive forms for creating and reviewing records.
Record detail views.
Confirmation and error feedback for important actions.
User profile/session controls.
Theme and display preferences may be supported.
